Getting here

For a taxi or Lyft/Uber, use the ISEC address directly:

805 Columbus Ave, Boston, MA 02115 →

Please don't use the generic address you find online for Northeastern as a whole; it points to the main campus, which is far from this location.

Walking route from Ruggles →

Coming from the Ruggles T station, follow the red arrows on the map. Exit down the stairs where the buses stop: ISEC is on the Columbus Ave side of the tracks, not the main-campus Huntington Ave side.
